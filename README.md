### Hi, I'm Rifah Muhaimina Rahman

I am a civil engineer from Bangladesh and an M.Sc. student in Integrated Water Resources Management at TH Köln, Germany. I work on hydrology, irrigation and floods using Earth observation data, hydrological models and machine learning, mostly for Bangladesh.

**Research interests:** hydrology and hydrometeorology · flood and drought risk · irrigation and agricultural water · remote sensing and machine learning · climate adaptation

---

#### Now

- Student research assistant at the Institute for Technology and Resources Management (ITT), TH Köln, in the Germany–Bangladesh **Web-AIS** project. I map Boro rice from Earth observation data in four pilot upazilas, with validation against 2,000+ georeferenced field samples.
- Co-authoring a manuscript (in preparation) on machine-learning-based evapotranspiration downscaling.

#### Before

- **Assistant Engineer, Bangladesh Agricultural Development Corporation (2022–2025).** Hydrological and irrigation data analysis, feasibility studies for development project proposals, irrigation efficiency, canal excavation, and waterlogging and flood programmes.
- **Geotechnical Engineer, ProSoil Foundation Consultant (2021–2022).** In-situ and laboratory testing for major infrastructure projects in Dhaka.
- **B.Sc. Civil Engineering, BUET (2021).**
